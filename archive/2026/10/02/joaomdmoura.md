### 🕐 12:00 UTC · @joaomdmoura

> My engineering team probably wishes I did not code. I still do. I love it. On a weekend I will get a crazy idea and think, let me try this out. What I do not do anymore is take tasks. I learned the hard way that if I try to do anything critical, I become a blocker. So I stay out of it. My ideas go in as PRs, and the team takes them from there and makes them look good. And there is still so much to improve. We ran one experiment that got all of our agents running five times faster. Five times. The fix was something so silly I had not seen it before. The coding also keeps a standard in my head. I use Cursor every day and I think its agent is amazing. My goal for CrewAI is that every agent anyone builds with it is that good by default. Founders, do you still write code? And who has to clean it up? Media
