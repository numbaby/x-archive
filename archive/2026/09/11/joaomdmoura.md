### 🕐 16:00 UTC · @joaomdmoura

> An agent matched human decisions 94% of the time. The 6% is the interesting part. A customer of ours has around 150,000 employees and a policy that lets teams request discounts. Marketing wants one for an event. Sales wants one to close a deal. The volume got so large globally that they had a dedicated team whose entire job was approving or denying those requests. Before trusting agents with it, they ran a backwards test. Last 30 days, what would the agent have approved or denied? 94% match with what the humans actually did. Their reaction was not let us automate everything. It was: if we can find the other 6% and route it to a person, we can roll this out. They now spend about 3% of the time they used to spend on it.
