### 🕐 17:34 UTC · @joaomdmoura

> Everyone tells you AI models are getting cheaper. The best ones are getting more expensive. There are two prices moving in opposite directions right now. The models that are not top quality keep getting cheaper. The cream of the crop keep going up. With GPT-5 we saw almost a 100% increase. They doubled it. Another frontier model changed its tokenizer, and that alone added 35 to 40% to what it costs to run. So what happens if only one of those two curves keeps going? You get two versions of the future. One where more people have access to this intelligence, and one where fewer do. That decides how much of this ends up controlled by the few, and how much by the many. Media
