### 🕐 17:14 UTC · @demishassabis

> The @GoogleDeepMind Science team is sharing two key cryptographic advances today uncovering weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA schemes. Working with leading cryptanalysis experts, our team showed how AI agents could make two significant cryptographic advances in just weeks, uncovering results which can bypass older digital signature protections and factor legacy 1024-bit RSA keys in bulk. While these results do not affect the security of modern encryption schemes such as RSA 2048, these results show why we must not assume the mathematical &quot;hardness&quot; of cryptographic schemes which we have relied upon in the past. It also shows how AI can help defenders improve security standards well ahead of future threats. I have written in more detail about our results, and why they are important for the future of security, in a longer essay which you can read here: https://x.com/pushmeet/status/2118752095859794390 https://x.com/i/article/2118746079923437568
