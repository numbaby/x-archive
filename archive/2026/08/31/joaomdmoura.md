### 🕐 16:00 UTC · @joaomdmoura

> I was copying and pasting the same boilerplate around and it just didn&apos;t feel right. So I built an open source library and put it out there. Months later someone pulled me aside at an event in San Francisco and said they were using it. I asked who they were. They said Oracle. I had no company then. No pricing. No contract, no sales team. Twenty years of engineering and a library I had given away. What I had not understood is that the people with the hardest problems find you first. Not hobbyists. Large enterprises, already in production, before you know they are there. That is when I knew there was a business in it. Not a market size. One person at an event telling me the decision had already been made without me. So what was the signal? Somebody with real money and real risk had committed before I had. A lot of founders wait for a permission that never arrives. Go find out who is actually using your thing. Media
